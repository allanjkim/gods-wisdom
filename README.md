# God's Wisdom Skill 🤝

This skill acts as an AI companion, offering empathetic guidance and life advice rooted in profound wisdom. It draws upon ancient scriptures and modern psychological insights to help users navigate challenges, find their purpose, and foster personal and spiritual growth.

Key Features
*   **Empathetic Listening**: Engage in supportive conversations tailored to your unique experiences and emotional state.
*   **Biblical Wisdom Integration**: Receive insightful advice grounded in scripture, with relevant verses provided to deepen understanding and offer comfort.
*   **Psychological Insights**: Benefit from guidance inspired by CBT principles and growth mindset philosophies, fostering resilience and personal development.
*   **Purpose Discovery**: Explore your strengths, values, and life's calling with thoughtful prompts and reflections.
*   **Community Connection**: Find inspiration and support by connecting with shared wisdom and user experiences (future feature).

Usage
Triggering the Skill
*   Life advice and guidance
*   Personal challenges and overcoming hardship
*   Spiritual questions and biblical interpretation
*   Finding purpose and self-discovery
*   Mental well-being and emotional support

The skill is triggered by keywords related to personal growth, spiritual matters, or life advice, especially those referencing God or Lord. Examples:
• "How to Pray to God" - "하나님께 기도하는 법"
• "Why Does the Lord Give Me Trials?" -  "주님은 왜 나에게 시련을 주시는가?"
• "I Want to Be Comforted by the Bible" -  "성경에서 위로받고 싶어"
• "Can you give me a Bible verse and advice for today, as I'm feeling anxious?" - "요즘 너무 불안한데, 오늘의 성경 한 구절 조언해 줄 수 있나요?" 

How it Works
The AI, embodying a wise and supportive mentor persona, analyzes user queries, draws upon biblical wisdom and psychological insights, and provides actionable advice with relevant scripture references.
